# **Role overview**

We are hiring a Social Media and Content Manager with strong writing ability and real technical curiosity.

Your primary focus will be building a credible, thoughtful founder presence online, supported by consistent and engaging company content. The work involves research, thinking, writing, community interaction and structured scheduling, not just posting for the sake of posting.

If you enjoy learning complex topics, forming opinions, and turning that thinking into content people actually want to read, this role will suit you.

## **About Ping Proxies**

Ping Proxies builds reliable infrastructure for teams that rely on proxies, scraping and networking at scale.

Our customers include web-scraping, agentic automation start-ups, developers, growth teams, and data companies who need stable, high-quality residential and ISP networks.

We are growing, our brand is becoming more visible, and we want our founder presence and company narrative to reflect the depth of what we actually do.

This role exists to make that happen.

## **Key responsibilities**

### **Founder content (primary focus)**

- Create frequent founder posts, primarily on LinkedIn and X.
- Produce original, well-researched content rather than generic templates.
- Explain technical topics clearly and accurately.
- Maintain the founder's authentic tone, perspective and opinion style.
- Find conversations, data points and insights worth contributing to.
- Repurpose long-form ideas into short threads, clips and posts without losing depth.

The goal is distinctive, credible, technically-informed content that adds value.

## **Company social and brand**

- Plan and schedule content across company channels.
- Create explainers, announcements, educational content and occasional memes from company accounts.
- Maintain consistency between the founder voice and company positioning.

## **Research and community engagement**

- Track developments across proxies, scraping, automation and infrastructure.
- Contribute thoughtfully in communities such as Reddit, Quora and relevant forums.
- Look for genuine opportunities to answer questions and add value.
- Use real discussions and questions as inspiration for future content.

## **Planning, scheduling and optimisation**

- Maintain a structured content calendar.
- Use scheduling tools such as Buffer, Hootsuite, Publer or Taplio.
- Review analytics and iterate based on performance rather than guesswork.
- Experiment gradually with new platforms such as Bluesky and Instagram where relevant.

## **What success looks like**

- Consistent founder posting with depth and originality.
- Growth in meaningful engagement, not just vanity metrics.
- Clearer positioning of Ping Proxies in our space.
- Stronger inbound interest and recognisable presence across communities.

# **Requirements**

## **Must have**

- Excellent written English with clarity and structure.
- Experience publishing content frequently while maintaining quality.
- Strong research habits and accuracy.
- Ability to ghostwrite and adapt tone convincingly.
- Comfort writing about technical topics such as proxies, scraping and infrastructure.
- Reliable, organised working style suitable for remote work.
- Ability to create simple visuals.
- Confident using Canva and Figma.

## **Nice to have**

- Experience in B2B SaaS, infrastructure, cybersecurity or networking.
- Previous work supporting founders or executives.
- Understanding of analytics across social platforms.

## **What we offer**

- Fully remote role.
- Direct collaboration with the founder.
- Ownership over narrative and storytelling.
- Room to experiment thoughtfully and improve over time.
